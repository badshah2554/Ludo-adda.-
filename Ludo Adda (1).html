Niyam

  • 6 aaye tabhi token yard se bahar nikalta hai.
  • 6, token kaatna ya ghar pahunchna par dobara phenko.
  • Star aur rang wale square safe hain, wahan token nahi katta.
  • Lagatar teen 6 aaye to baari chali jaati hai.
  • Jiske sabhi token pehle beech mein pahunche, wahi jeetega.
  • Rush Mode mein har khiladi ke sirf 2 token hote hain, isliye game jaldi khatam hota hai.
